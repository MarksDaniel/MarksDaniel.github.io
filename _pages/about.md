  <section class="landing-section">
    <h2>Research focus</h2>
    <p class="landing-research-summary">My research lies at the intersection of statistical learning theory and Bayesian statistics and focuses on equipping data-driven learning algorithms and inference procedures with performance and safety guarantees. My work has been presented at leading academic conferences and includes an oral presentation and a <a href="https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/marks25a.html">Best Student Paper Award</a> at <a href="https://aistats.org/aistats2025//awards.html">AISTATS 2025</a>.</p>

    <p class="landing-section-intro">A recurring theme in my work is that such guarantees can arise in two complementary ways:</p>

    <dl class="research-principles">
      <div class="research-principle">
        <dt>Guarantees by design</dt>
        <dd>Developing prescriptive training procedures that turn flexible black-box models into certifiable learning algorithms with strong post-training guarantees.</dd>
      </div>

      <div class="research-principle">
        <dt>Guarantees by self-certification</dt>
        <dd>Identifying learning and inference methods whose own structure or training dynamics already provide the ingredients for strong theoretical guarantees.</dd>
      </div>
    </dl>

    <p class="landing-outlook">My goal is to apply this philosophy to questions relating to generalisation in deep learning, certifiable online and continual learning, and approximate Bayesian inference, particularly variational inference.</p>

  </section>
